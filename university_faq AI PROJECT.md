# Sample University - FAQ Knowledge Base
(Note: this is sample data. Replace with your own university's documents.)

## Admissions

Q: What is the eligibility for MCA admission?
A: Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree (BCA/BSc/BCom/BA with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level) with at least 50% marks (45% for reserved categories). Admission is based on a valid entrance test score followed by counselling.

Q: How do I apply for admission?
A: Applications are submitted online through the university admission portal. Create an account, fill in the application form, upload scanned documents (mark sheets, ID proof, photograph, signature) and pay the application fee online.

Q: What is the last date to apply for admission?
A: The last date for the main round is 30 June every year. A late round may be announced in July with a late fee of Rs 500.

Q: Which documents are required at the time of admission?
A: Original and photocopies of 10th and 12th mark sheets, graduation mark sheets, entrance test scorecard, character certificate, migration certificate, category certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card and six passport size photographs.

## Fees

Q: What is the fee structure for the MCA programme?
A: The MCA tuition fee is Rs 60,000 per semester. In addition there is a one-time admission fee of Rs 5,000 and a refundable security deposit of Rs 5,000.

Q: How can I pay my fees?
A: Fees can be paid online through UPI, net banking or debit/credit card on the student portal, or offline by demand draft in favour of the university payable at the main campus.

Q: Can I pay the fees in instalments?
A: Yes. Semester fees can be paid in two instalments: 60% at the time of registration and 40% within 45 days. A fine of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date.

Q: What is the refund policy if I withdraw admission?
A: If you withdraw before the commencement of classes, 90% of the fees are refunded. Between 1 and 15 days after classes start, 50% is refunded. No refund is given after 15 days.

## Scholarships

Q: What scholarships are available for students?
A: The university offers a merit scholarship (25% tuition waiver for students scoring above 85% in the previous exam), a sports scholarship, and need-based aid. Government scholarships for SC/ST/OBC students are also facilitated through the scholarship cell.

Q: How do I apply for a scholarship?
A: Apply through the scholarship section of the student portal within 30 days of admission. Attach income certificate, category certificate and previous mark sheets. Results are declared within 4 weeks.

## Hostel and Accommodation

Q: Does the university provide hostel facilities?
A: Yes. Separate hostels for boys and girls are available on campus with Wi-Fi, 24x7 security, laundry and a mess. Hostel seats are allotted on a first come first served basis after admission.

Q: What are the hostel fees?
A: The hostel fee is Rs 45,000 per year for a double sharing room and Rs 65,000 per year for a single room. Mess charges are Rs 3,500 per month.

Q: What are the hostel timings?
A: Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM for girls and 10:30 PM for boys. Late entry needs prior written permission from the warden.

## Examinations

Q: How many semesters are there in the MCA programme?
A: MCA is a two-year programme divided into four semesters. Each semester has about 90 teaching days.

Q: What is the minimum attendance required to appear in exams?
A: Students must have at least 75% attendance in each subject. Students with 65-75% attendance may appear with a condonation fee of Rs 1,000 and valid medical grounds.

Q: How is internal assessment conducted?
A: Each theory subject has 30 marks for internal assessment (two class tests, assignments and attendance) and 70 marks for the end semester exam. Practical subjects are evaluated 50:50 between internal and external examiners.

Q: What is the passing criteria?
A: A student must score at least 40% in each subject, and a minimum CGPA of 5.0 is needed to pass the semester.

Q: How can I apply for re-evaluation of my answer sheet?
A: Apply on the student portal within 10 days of the result declaration by paying Rs 500 per subject. Revised results are published within 3 weeks.

Q: When are the semester examinations held?
A: Odd semester exams are held in December and even semester exams are held in May. The date sheet is released one month in advance on the university website.

## Library

Q: What are the library timings?
A: The central library is open from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Q: How many books can I borrow from the library?
A: Postgraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. A fine of Rs 2 per day is charged for late return. The library also provides access to e-journals and digital databases.

## Placements

Q: Does the university provide placement support?
A: Yes. The Training and Placement Cell conducts aptitude training, mock interviews and campus drives. Over 150 companies visit every year, and the average package last year was 6.2 LPA.

Q: Is there an internship requirement in MCA?
A: Yes. Students must complete a 6-month industry project or internship in the fourth semester, followed by a project report and viva voce.

## Student Services

Q: How do I get my student ID card?
A: ID cards are issued by the admission office within 15 days of admission. For a lost ID card, submit an application with a fee of Rs 200 for a duplicate.

Q: How can I get a bonafide or character certificate?
A: Apply on the student portal under the certificates section or visit the administrative office. Certificates are issued within 3 working days for a fee of Rs 100.

Q: How do I contact the university help desk?
A: You can email helpdesk@sampleuniversity.edu.in or call 0000-000000 between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM on working days. The help desk is located in the administrative block.

Q: Does the university have a anti-ragging policy?
A: Yes. Ragging is strictly prohibited and punishable. Students can report incidents to the anti-ragging committee or call the national helpline 1800-180-5522, which is available 24x7.

Q: Are there Wi-Fi and computer lab facilities?
A: The entire campus has Wi-Fi. Computer labs are open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM and have licensed software, high-speed internet and GPU workstations for AI/ML projects.
